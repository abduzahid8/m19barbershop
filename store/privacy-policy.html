M19 Barbershop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. App version 1.1.0. Contact: info@m19barbershop.uz, +998 91 004 00 19.

1. No accounts, no tracking

The app requires no login and includes no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your data.

2. Data stored on your device only

  • Language preference (RU / UZ / EN) — stored locally via app storage.
  • Cached public reviews — copies of our public Yandex Maps reviews, cached locally so the app works offline.

This data never leaves your device except as described below.

3. Booking via Altegio (Alteg.io)

Online booking opens the Altegio booking website (n129791.alteg.io) inside the app. Any name, phone number, or booking details you enter there are processed directly by Altegio under their own privacy terms in order to fulfil your appointment (App Store purpose: App Functionality). We receive your booking through Altegio as the service provider.

4. Public reviews via Yandex

The app displays our public reviews from Yandex Maps, loaded through our backend relay. This is read-only: we fetch the public author name, rating, text, date, and avatar URL. No Yandex credentials or private data are accessed.

5. Links that leave the app

Tapping call, Telegram (t.me/M19barbershop), Instagram, Yandex Maps, or our website (m19.uz) opens the phone dialer, browser, or the corresponding app. Their own privacy policies then apply.

6. App Privacy (App Store Connect) disclosure

  • Contact Info — Name, Phone Number: provided by you when booking via Altegio; linked to you; used for App Functionality; not used for tracking.
  • Data Not Collected: no precise location, no contacts, no photos, no identifiers collected by the app itself.
  • Tracking: none (NSPrivacyTracking = false).

7. Encryption

All network traffic uses HTTPS. The app uses only standard OS encryption and is exempt from export-compliance documentation (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false).

8. Children

The app is a general-audience barbershop booking app, rated 4+. It is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and take effect on publication.
